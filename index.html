The House & Surroundings

12 Guests  ·  5 Bedrooms  ·  3 Bathrooms  ·  Private Pool

Nestled in the hills of Castel Boglione, Casa del Lupino Bianco offers a private retreat in the heart of Piedmont's UNESCO World Heritage wine region. Spend your days exploring wineries, local markets and picturesque villages, discovering the region's renowned truffles and culinary traditions, or simply relaxing by the pool and gathering for long dinners under the pergola.

Explore Piedmont

From world-famous wines and white truffles to thermal spas and historic cities — the region surrounding the house is endlessly rich.

Nizza Monferrato

  • Visit the Distillerie Berta, where the world-renowned Berta Grappa is produced.
  • Explore the Palazzo Comunale e Torre Civica, the historic town hall and civic tower at the heart of the town.
  • Browse the antique market held on the third Sunday of each month — many of the furnishings in Casa del Lupino Bianco were sourced from this wonderful market.

Asti

  • Explore the historic city center and the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta.
  • Enjoy wine tasting, especially the famous Asti Spumante.
  • Do not miss the Palio di Asti in September, one of the oldest horse races in Italy.

Turin

  • Visit the National Cinema Museum and take the elevator to the top for stunning panoramic views of the city.
  • Visit Sacra di San Michele, a breathtaking mountaintop abbey said to have inspired Umberto Eco's The Name of the Rose.
  • Stroll along Via Roma and enjoy shopping and cafes.

Cuneo

  • Visit the beautiful Cathedral of Santa Maria del Bosco.
  • Join the Fiera Nazionale del Marrone festival in October, celebrating the region's chestnut harvest.
  • The Sagra della Nocciola di Cortemilia celebrates the famous hazelnut spread that inspired Nutella. It takes place each August in the picturesque town of Cortemilia.

Alba

  • Visit the famous white truffle fair at the Cortile della Maddalena from early October to late November.
  • Participate in a truffle searching trip with trained truffle dogs.
  • Visit local wineries and taste the famous Barolo and Barbaresco wines.

Acqui Terme

  • Get a spa treatment in the town's famous hot springs, such as the Lago delle Sorgenti or the Terme di Acqui.
  • Stop by the Bollente hot spring fountain, where visitors often fill bottles with the thermal water, believed to have healing properties.
  • Explore the remains of the ancient Roman aqueduct, which once supplied water to the town.

Alessandria

  • Visit the impressive Citadel of Alessandria, a star-shaped fortress built in the 18th century.
  • Enjoy shopping in Alessandria's boutiques, known for high-quality leather goods.
  • Join local festivals, such as the Fiera di San Giorgio in April.

Casale Monferrato

  • Enjoy outdoor activities in the Po River Park, which offers walking and cycling trails.
  • Explore the Festa del Vino in September, the famous wine festival.
